# Contributing

This repository is the reading and the site. The trainer and the market files are not here, and a pull request cannot add them.

An issue is welcome when a sentence is wrong, a page is broken, or a citation does not point where it should. Say which page, which sentence, and what you expected to see.

Do not attach market data, vendor files, or account records. Those do not belong in a public issue, and they will not be used.

Be kind. The pages are a record of a finished study, so a change to the counts or the paths needs a reason a reader can check. Response time will vary.
